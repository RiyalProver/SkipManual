# White-site redesign and complete examples

## Visitor priorities

1. Understand within the opening screen that SkipManual builds local-business websites and customer follow-up systems for $249/month.
2. See a relevant business website immediately, with direct access to the full example.
3. Understand all five included services through ordinary situations and concise explanations.
4. Reach booking or a short inquiry from the header, page content, and mobile action bar.

## Build scope

- White agency canvas, orange calls to action, readable type, photographic business imagery, and straightforward navigation.
- Reworked homepage, services overview, individual service pages, how-it-works page, pricing, example collection, and booking page.
- Ten pages for each of the five example businesses. Add service depth, galleries, visitor guides, FAQs, and contextual inquiry links.
- Keep each example's visual identity; use new local WebP photography and gallery enlargement with keyboard support.
- Shorten the agency inquiry to essential fields, with optional details disclosed separately. Carry service or industry context into the form.
- Connect a verified booking link and contact email when provided. Until then, clearly identify the locally prepared request without inventing appointments or claiming delivery.
- Preserve unique metadata and real SkipManual business schema. Fictional example pages remain noindex and do not carry real-business schema.

## Verification

Build every route, check links/images and responsive layout at four widths, exercise booking configuration, request handoffs, menus, galleries, FAQs, filters, and no-JavaScript behavior. Review actual desktop and phone captures and refresh portfolio screenshots.
